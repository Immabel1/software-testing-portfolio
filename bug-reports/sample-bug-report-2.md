Instagram

Title: Story upload stuck at 99%

App Version: 331.0.0
Device: iPhone 13
OS: iOS 18

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open Instagram.
2. Create a story with a photo and music sticker.
3. Tap “Your Story” to upload.

## Actual Result

The upload progress stops at 99% and never completes until the app is restarted.
It happens randomly, mostly on cellular data and the same photo uploads correctly when the music sticker is removed.

## Expected Result

The story should upload successfully within a few seconds with the added music.
