Calculator App

Title: Decimal calculation gives incorrect rounding result

App Version: 4.8.1

Device: Google Pixel 7

OS: Android 14

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Calculator app.
2. Enter: 10 ÷ 3
3. Copy the result and paste it into another calculation.
4. Multiply by 3.

## Actual Result

The app displays 9.999999 instead of 10

## Expected Result

The final answer should equal exactly 10.
