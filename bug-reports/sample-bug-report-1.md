Title: Video "diy cheap pizza" freezes when switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data.

App Version:19.21.3

Device: Samsung Galaxy A54

OS:Android 14

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open YouTube 
2. Navigate to "cakes and pastes" channel.
3. Play the "diy cheap pizza" video from the "quick recipes" playlist. 
4. Start watching on Wi-Fi.
5. Turn off Wi-Fi so the phone switches to mobile data.
6. Continue watching the video.

## Actual Result

The video freezes for about 10–15 seconds and sometimes shows an infinite loading icon. The issue does not happen when starting the video directly on mobile data.

## Expected Result

The video should continue playing without interruption. It shouldn't just keep loading or freeze.
